GLOSSARY
NICE Constitutional System — v0

This glossary defines terms as they are used constitutionally in NICE.
Definitions are normative.
Where a common word is used in a constrained way, that constraint is explicit.

⸻

Authority
The exclusive capacity to originate intent, grant consent, authorize execution, and hold standing.
In NICE, authority originates once — with the human — and nowhere else.

⸻

Boundary
A constitutional enforcement edge that constrains behavior and limits what may occur across it.
Boundaries enforce rules; they do not execute actions.

⸻

Commons
An intentional coordination structure governed by declared constraints.
Commons enable collective participation without originating authority.

⸻

Commons Governance Boundary
The constitutional boundary that enforces collective rules, scope, and accountability without granting autonomous authority to the collective.

⸻

Conformance
Declared alignment with a specified standard or constraint set.
Conformance is inspectable, revocable, and does not confer authority.

⸻

Constitutional
Pertaining to the rules that define where authority originates, how participation is constrained, and what systems may not do — regardless of implementation.

⸻

Continuity
Preservation of contextual coherence over time without inference, prediction, or optimization.
Continuity maintains alignment with the human without modeling or acting.

⸻

Declaration
A non-executable statement of intent, constraint, eligibility, or availability.
Declarations are inspected and reconciled; they do not cause execution.

⸻

Declared Participation Mediation
The constitutional plane where declarations are evaluated for compatibility and eligibility without executing or routing actions.

⸻

Eligibility
A determination of whether declared criteria are satisfied.
Eligibility is evaluated, not granted, and carries no discretion.

⸻

Execution
The performance of an action that causes effects in the world or system.
Execution is always downstream of authorization and never constitutional.

⸻

Governance
Enforcement of declared constraints and accountability conditions without discretionary control.
Governance constrains behavior; it does not direct outcomes.

⸻

Human Authority & Personal Trust
The singular constitutional plane where human intent, consent, custody, authorization, and interpretive context originate.

⸻

Interpretive Assistance
Human-instigated support that renders options, tradeoffs, and implications legible without initiating action, narrowing choice, or making decisions.

⸻

Mediation
Reconciliation of declarations without authority transfer or execution.
Mediation evaluates compatibility; it does not coordinate actions.

⸻

Participation
A bounded engagement in a declared activity under explicit constraints.
Participation is always voluntary and human-authorized.

⸻

Participation Availability
A declared statement that participation is possible under specified constraints.
Availability is visibility, not permission.

⸻

Participation Visibility
The condition under which participation availability may be observed by potential participants.
Visibility does not imply routing, delivery, or obligation.

⸻

Personal Trust Boundary
The constitutional enforcement boundary that protects human authority, custody, and authorization.
No action may originate across this boundary without explicit human involvement.

⸻

Platform
A replaceable implementation that enforces constitutional constraints without owning authority, intent, or discretion.

⸻

PeelPack
The unit of software delivery in NICE: a peelable package consisting of a non-executable, inspectable envelope and its contents. Participation offerings ride in PeelPacks; contents are verified on custody read; unwrapping is receipted.
bApp (retired)
Former name of the bounded-capability package noun. Replaced by PeelPack as of v0.1.4. Historical occurrences in frozen design documents stand as history.

⌻

Sensemaking
The human process of interpreting meaning, context, and implications.
In NICE, systems may assist sensemaking but may not perform it autonomously.

⸻

Subordinate Assistance
System-provided support that operates strictly under human initiation and approval.
Subordinate assistance never substitutes judgment or initiates action.

⸻

TIX (Trusted Interchange)
The Trusted Interchange of the NICE platform system: the mediation plane where attested estates mutually give and receive. Every crossing is verified and recorded. The interchange is trusted-of - participants attested - never trusting: it never vouches, decides, holds custody, or acts unilaterally.
TIX-MEDIATION
The singular mediation boundary of the TIX. It verifies declared envelopes, reconciles eligibility, and attests crossings - publishing outcomes to the journal without executing, governing, originating authority, or holding custody.

⌻

Trust Boundary
A constitutional or platform boundary where enforcement occurs without authority origination.

⸻

Visibility
The condition of being observable without being delivered, pushed, or acted upon.

⸻

End of Glossary
