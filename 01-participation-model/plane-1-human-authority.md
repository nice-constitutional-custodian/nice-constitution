Plane 1 — Human Authority & Personal Trust

Constitutional Plane — Singular

Purpose

This document defines the absolute origin of authority in NICE.

It establishes the human as the sole source of intent, consent, custody, authorization, and interpretive context, and defines the constitutional conditions under which participation may occur — without delegation, automation, or substitution of human standing.

This plane exists once — and only once.

All other constitutional planes, platforms, declarations, and execution environments inherit constraints from this boundary.

⸻

Authority originates once — and only once

In NICE, authority originates with the individual human.

It is expressed through a singular constitutional boundary that precedes all platforms, collectives, products, and implementations.

No system, platform, collective, or process may:
	•	originate authority,
	•	reinterpret human intent,
	•	infer objectives,
	•	initiate action, or
	•	act ahead of human observation.

Human authority is not delegated, replicated, pooled, or abstracted.
It is declared, reviewable, and revocable — or it does not exist.

⸻

What originates at this plane

The Human Authority & Personal Trust plane is the sole constitutional origin of:
	•	Intent — what the human seeks to do or participate in
	•	Consent — explicit permission to participate under defined conditions
	•	Custody — control over identity, data, and commitments
	•	Authorization — permission to allow bounded execution downstream
	•	Interpretive context — the meaning and framing the human brings to participation

These originate once, at the human boundary, and nowhere else.

No downstream system may manufacture, infer, or override these elements.

⸻

Continuity with the human (the alignment substrate)

This plane includes a continuous alignment substrate that remains in lock-step with the human.

Its role is to preserve coherence between:
	•	declared intent,
	•	lived digital interaction,
	•	and future acts of participation.

This substrate is analogous to an operating system:
	•	it maintains continuity and readiness,
	•	keeps internal state aligned,
	•	and ensures the system is prepared to act when — and only when — a command is issued.

It does not:
	•	infer goals,
	•	interpret meaning,
	•	optimize behavior,
	•	initiate action, or
	•	make decisions.

Alignment preserves continuity with the human; it does not substitute judgment.

⸻

Assistance without delegation

Human understanding may be supported by explicit, human-invoked assistance, but never replaced.

Interpretive or sensemaking assistance:
	•	may render options legible,
	•	may surface implications or tradeoffs,
	•	may help the human reflect or decide,

but it never:
	•	narrows choice without consent,
	•	initiates action,
	•	executes on behalf of the human,
	•	or substitutes judgment.

Assistance is subordinate by design.

⸻

What is constitutionally impossible at this plane

The following are explicitly disallowed at the Human Authority & Personal Trust plane:
	•	Silent automation
	•	Background optimization
	•	Behavioral profiling
	•	Inferred intent or objectives
	•	Push-based execution
	•	Delegated authority
	•	Subconscious control loops

Nothing may act for the human.
Nothing may act ahead of the human.
Nothing may act without the human.

⸻

Relationship to downstream planes

This plane does not execute participation.

It supplies:
	•	declared intent,
	•	declared constraints,
	•	and bounded authorization

to downstream constitutional mediation and governed collective participation.

Evaluation, matching, routing, and execution — if they occur at all — are always downstream, always bounded, and always revocable.

⸻

Constitutional invariant

The Human Authority & Personal Trust plane exists to ensure that:
	•	Human agency is never absorbed by systems
	•	Participation is always explicit
	•	Alignment never becomes control
	•	Authority remains singular and human

This boundary protects humans not from systems —
but from their own cognitive limits being exploited by systems.

Nothing more — and nothing less.
