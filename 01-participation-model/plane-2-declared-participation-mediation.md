Plane 2 — Declared Participation Mediation

Constitutional Plane — Singular

Purpose

This document defines how declared participation is reconciled in NICE without granting authority, initiating execution, or exercising discretion.

This plane exists to answer one question only:

Given declared intent, consent, constraints, and availability — is participation compatible?

It evaluates, reconciles, and publishes conditions for possible participation.
It does not cause participation to occur.

⸻

Role in the Constitutional System

Declared Participation Mediation sits between:
	•	the Human Authority & Personal Trust plane, where authority originates, and
	•	Governed Collective Participation, where collective constraints apply.

This plane is:
	•	singular at the constitutional level,
	•	plural in implementation,
	•	and strictly non-executing.

It is a mediation plane, not a control plane.

⸻

What this plane does

This plane reconciles declarations that originate elsewhere.

Specifically, it:
	•	evaluates declared intent and consent,
	•	evaluates declared eligibility and conformance,
	•	reconciles declared participation availability,
	•	and determines whether participation may be possible under declared constraints.

It produces visibility, not action.

⸻

What this plane does not do

Declared Participation Mediation never:
	•	originates authority
	•	grants permission
	•	selects execution venues
	•	initiates execution
	•	routes commands
	•	interprets intent
	•	exercises discretion

Compatibility is determined here.
Action happens elsewhere — if at all.

⸻

Declaration types handled

This plane evaluates and reconciles the following declaration types:
	•	Participation Declaration
What a human or collective is willing to participate in.
	•	Conformance Declaration
What standards, roles, or requirements are claimed or satisfied.
	•	Eligibility Declaration
Whether participation conditions are met under declared rules.
	•	Participation Availability Declaration
Whether a party is open to participation under declared scope.
(Availability is not permission and not routing.)
	•	Commitment & Provenance Declaration
What obligations, receipts, or outcomes may be recorded.

Each declaration is:
	•	declarative, not executable
	•	inspectable, not authoritative
	•	pull-based, not pushed as instruction

⸻

Evaluation without execution

All mediation performed at this plane is evaluative only.

This means:
	•	declarations are compared, not acted upon
	•	compatibility is assessed, not enforced
	•	participation may be visible, but never initiated

Execution decisions — including whether to act, where to act, or how to act — are always made downstream, under human or collective authority.

⸻

Availability, not routing

This plane may publish participation availability.

Availability means:
	•	participation could occur under declared conditions
	•	no party is obligated to act
	•	no execution path is selected
	•	no instruction is issued

Routing, invitation, polling, or execution selection are explicitly out of scope for this plane.

⸻

Physical realization disclaimer

While participation compatibility is evaluated constitutionally at this plane, physical realization occurs elsewhere.

In practice:
	•	humans act through PERS, in declared roles,
	•	collectives act through governed structures,
	•	platforms observe and pull declarations.

This plane does not prescribe how realization occurs — only whether it is compatible.

⸻

Relationship to Constitutional Declarations

Declared Participation Mediation operates exclusively over constitutional declarations.
It does not interpret meaning, infer intent, or execute behavior.
Its role is to inspect, reconcile, and align declarations that have been explicitly authored elsewhere.

The following declaration types are evaluated within this plane:
	•	Participation Availability Declaration
		Signals that a bounded capability (e.g., a bApp) is available for potential participation.
		Availability is declarative only; it does not grant permission, select recipients, or imply routing.
	•	Participation Visibility Declaration
		Constrains who may observe participation availability and under what conditions.
		Visibility governs notice, not access or execution.
	•	Conformance Declaration
		Declares adherence to required rules, interfaces, or governance constraints.
		Conformance is inspected, not enforced, at this plane.
	•	Eligibility Declaration
		Declares the criteria under which participation would be acceptable.
		Eligibility is evaluated structurally; no discretion or prioritization occurs here.
	•	Commitment & Provenance Declaration
		Declares binding commitments and records accountable outcomes.
		Provenance is preserved without introducing authority or control.

Declared Participation Mediation reconciles these declarations for compatibility and constraint alignment only.

No declaration within this plane may initiate action, grant permission, or determine execution paths.

Execution, routing, and authority remain outside this plane by constitutional design.

⸻

Constitutional invariant

Declared Participation Mediation exists to ensure that:
	•	participation is explicit, not implicit
	•	compatibility is visible, not coercive
	•	coordination occurs without authority transfer
	•	execution never hides inside mediation

This plane makes participation legible without becoming active.

Nothing more — and nothing less.
