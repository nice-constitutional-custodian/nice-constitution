Eligibility Declaration (v0)

Purpose

The Eligibility Declaration defines the conditions under which a potential participant is compatible with a participation offering.

It answers the question:

If a human (or human-represented role) chooses to evaluate participation, are they eligible under the declared constraints?

This declaration exists to support evaluation without authority, enabling compatibility checks while preserving human control and decision-making.

⸻

What This Declaration Declares

An Eligibility Declaration declares:
	•	The criteria for participation compatibility, such as:
	•	Role requirements
	•	Credential requirements
	•	Jurisdictional constraints
	•	Capability or resource prerequisites
	•	Any exclusion conditions that disqualify participation
	•	Whether eligibility is:
	•	Binary (eligible / not eligible)
	•	Conditional (eligible if additional conditions are met)

Eligibility is a declaration about compatibility, not approval.

⸻

What This Declaration Does Not Do

This declaration does not:
	•	Grant permission to participate
	•	Select or prioritize participants
	•	Initiate participation
	•	Route execution
	•	Allocate resources
	•	Express intent by any participant
	•	Override human judgment

Eligibility is not authorization.

⸻

Relationship to Other Declarations

The Eligibility Declaration works in coordination with:
	•	Participation Visibility Declaration
(where the offering may be observed)
	•	Participation Availability Declaration
(whether the offering is currently open for consideration)
	•	Conformance Declaration
(whether implementations meet declared standards)

Eligibility is evaluated only after visibility and independent of availability.

⸻

Evaluation Semantics
	•	Eligibility is evaluated, not enforced
	•	Evaluation may be:
	•	Performed locally
	•	Performed through declared mediation
	•	Repeated as conditions change
	•	Evaluation does not imply:
	•	Consent
	•	Obligation
	•	Participation
	•	Execution

No system may treat eligibility as a mandate to act.

⸻

Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants apply:
	•	Eligibility does not imply permission
	•	Eligibility does not imply availability
	•	Eligibility does not imply execution
	•	Eligibility never transfers authority
	•	Eligibility does not narrow human choice

A participant may be eligible and still choose not to participate.

⸻

Example (Non-Normative)

A Commons may declare that a data-sharing bApp:
	•	Requires a specific role (e.g., Steward)
	•	Requires possession of a credential
	•	Excludes certain jurisdictions

This declaration:
	•	Defines compatibility conditions
	•	Does not approve participation
	•	Does not initiate execution
	•	Does not bind the human to act

All decisions remain with the human participant.

⸻

v0 Completion Criteria

This declaration is considered v0 complete when:
	•	Eligibility is clearly distinguished from permission and execution
	•	No language implies obligation or routing
	•	The declaration survives a hostile read without being interpreted as control
