Constitutional Declarations (v0)

Purpose

Constitutional Declarations are normative statements that define how participation in NICE is observed, evaluated, constrained, and accounted for — without executing, authorizing, or controlling behavior.

They exist to make participation inspectable and accountable while preserving human authority.

Declarations are not instructions.
They are not executable logic.
They do not initiate action.

⸻

What a Declaration Is

In NICE, a declaration is:
	•	Declarative — it states facts, constraints, or claims
	•	Inspectable — it can be reviewed and evaluated
	•	Pulled / observed — never pushed as authority
	•	Non-executable — it does not cause action

Declarations define conditions, not outcomes.

⸻

What Declarations Do Not Do

No constitutional declaration may:
	•	Grant permission
	•	Initiate execution
	•	Route commands
	•	Infer intent or goals
	•	Narrow human choice
	•	Transfer authority

Any system that treats declarations as commands is non-conformant.

⸻

Declaration Set (v0)

The v0 constitutional declaration set consists of the following artifacts:

1. Participation Visibility Declaration

File: participation-visibility-declaration.md

Declares where and how a participation offering may be observed.
	•	Defines discoverability
	•	Defines visibility scope
	•	Does not imply invitation, availability, or permission

Visibility answers:
Where can this be seen?

⸻

2. Participation Availability Declaration

File: participation-availability-declaration.md

Declares whether a participation offering is open for consideration at a given time or state.
	•	Defines openness
	•	May include temporal or capacity constraints
	•	Does not imply permission, eligibility, or execution

Availability answers:
Is this open to be evaluated right now?

⸻

3. Eligibility Declaration

File: eligibility-declaration.md

Declares compatibility conditions for participation.
	•	Defines who could participate
	•	May include role, credential, jurisdiction, or capability constraints
	•	Does not select participants or authorize action

Eligibility answers:
Is this compatible with me, if I choose to evaluate it?

⸻

4. Conformance Declaration

File: conformance-declaration.md

Declares claimed compliance with standards.
	•	States which standards are claimed
	•	May include scope or exclusions
	•	Does not certify correctness or grant trust

Conformance answers:
What standards does this claim to meet?

⸻

5. Commitment & Provenance Declaration

File: commitment-and-provenance-declaration.md

Declares what commitments were made and what occurred.
	•	Records outcomes and evidence
	•	Supports audit and accountability
	•	Applies after participation, not before

Provenance answers:
What happened, and how can it be inspected later?

⸻

Relationship Between Declarations

Declarations are complementary, not sequential commands.

Conceptually:
	•	Visibility defines where observation may occur
	•	Availability defines whether evaluation is timely
	•	Eligibility defines compatibility
	•	Conformance informs evaluation
	•	Commitment & provenance support accountability

No declaration implies execution or ordering.

⸻

Constitutional Invariants

Across all declarations, the following invariants hold:
	•	Declarations are not authority
	•	Declarations are not permission
	•	Declarations are not routing
	•	Declarations are not execution
	•	Declarations never replace human judgment

Authority originates with humans — once, and only once.

⸻

v0 Completion Criteria

The declaration set is v0 complete when:
	•	Each declaration has a clear, non-overlapping role
	•	No declaration leaks execution or authority semantics
	•	The set survives a hostile read without contradiction

This criterion is satisfied for v0.
