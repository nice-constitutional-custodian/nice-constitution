Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Boundary

Constitutional Boundary — Singular

Purpose

This document defines the Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Boundary:
the constitutional boundary where human-declared intent, consent, custody, authorization, and interpretive context are evaluated for permissibility — without delegation of authority or initiation of action.

This boundary exists to protect human standing by ensuring that:
	•	nothing acts without human declaration,
	•	nothing evaluates eligibility without human authority,
	•	and nothing substitutes judgment for the human.

Relationship to the Constitutional Planes

The Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Boundary is the enforcement boundary of the Human Authority & Personal Trust plane.

It is the only boundary where:
	•	authority originates,
	•	eligibility is evaluated relative to the human,
	•	and declared constraints are enforced at the point of entry.

All other constitutional boundaries depend on this one.

⸻

Authority originates once — and only once

Human authority originates with the human and nowhere else.

This boundary is the sole constitutional origin of:
	•	intent,
	•	consent,
	•	custody,
	•	authorization,
	•	and interpretive context.

No platform, system, collective, or process may:
	•	originate authority,
	•	reinterpret intent,
	•	infer goals,
	•	initiate action,
	•	or act ahead of human observation.

Authority is declared here — once, and only once.

⸻

Evaluation without origination

The Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Boundary enforces declared constraints.
It does not originate meaning or action.

Specifically, it:
	•	evaluates declared intent,
	•	checks eligibility against declared conditions,
	•	enforces custody and authorization boundaries,
	•	and constrains what execution may be permitted elsewhere.

It does not:
	•	decide outcomes,
	•	select actions,
	•	or initiate execution.

Evaluation answers “may this proceed?” — never “what should happen?”

⸻

Assistance without delegation

Interpretive assistance is constitutionally permitted at this boundary.

Such assistance:
	•	supports human understanding,
	•	surfaces options, tradeoffs, and implications,
	•	and reduces cognitive load.

It never:
	•	narrows choice,
	•	substitutes judgment,
	•	initiates action,
	•	or produces decisions.

Assistance remains subordinate to the human at all times.

⸻

Continuity with the human

This boundary maintains continuity with the human across time and interaction.

Continuity means:
	•	declared context remains coherent,
	•	prior declarations remain inspectable,
	•	and participation remains intelligible to the human.

This continuity:
	•	does not infer intent,
	•	does not optimize behavior,
	•	does not act subconsciously,
	•	and does not anticipate goals.

Alignment preserves continuity with the human;
it does not infer goals, optimize behavior, or act ahead of observation.

⸻

Custody without execution

Custody of identity and data is enforced at this boundary.

This includes:
	•	human-originated data,
	•	third-party data entrusted under declared terms,
	•	and derived artifacts subject to retention constraints.

Execution may occur elsewhere — but only within envelopes declared and enforced here.

Custody and execution are constitutionally separated by design.

⸻

Singular boundary, universal dependency

The Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Boundary is singular.

All participation — personal, collective, or institutional — ultimately depends on it.

No collective may bypass it.
No platform may replace it.
No optimization may obscure it.

⸻

Constitutional invariant

The Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Boundary guarantees that:
	•	authority is explicit,
	•	eligibility is evaluated, not assumed,
	•	assistance does not become control,
	•	and participation remains voluntary, inspectable, and human-led.

Nothing happens without the human —
and nothing happens to the human without declaration.
