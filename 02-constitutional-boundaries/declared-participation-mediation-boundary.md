IX Mediation Boundary

Declaration Evaluation & Eligibility Matching Boundary (Constitutional)

Purpose

The IX Mediation Boundary defines the constitutional surface where declared participation is reconciled across multiple humans, personas, collectives, and execution contexts without transferring authority, intent, or standing.

This boundary enables plural participation to interoperate while preserving the invariant that authority originates only with humans and execution remains downstream.

IX is a mediation surface — not a control plane.

Constitutional Role

The IX Mediation Boundary:
	•	evaluates declared participation,
	•	reconciles compatibility across contexts,
	•	matches eligibility and permitted execution scopes, and
	•	publishes declared availability of participation offerings,

without:
	•	executing actions,
	•	granting permission,
	•	issuing commands,
	•	interpreting meaning,
	•	or originating authority.

Participation is mediated here — never enacted.

⸻

What This Boundary Does

Within this boundary, IX:
	•	inspects declared participation envelopes,
	•	evaluates conformance against declared constraints,
	•	determines eligibility across personas, Commons, and contexts,
	•	reconciles compatible execution boundaries,
	•	publishes declared availability of PeelPacks,
	•	records receipts, attestations, and provenance artifacts.

All actions are inspectable, auditable, and non-authoritative.

⸻

What This Boundary Does Not Do

The IX Mediation Boundary never:
	•	initiates execution,
	•	routes commands,
	•	grants permission,
	•	interprets intent,
	•	infers goals,
	•	optimizes behavior,
	•	or acts on behalf of a human or collective.

IX expresses conditions, not decisions.

⸻

Declaration Types Handled

This boundary inspects and reconciles the following constitutional declaration types:

Participation Declaration

Declares:
	•	intent to participate,
	•	role and scope,
	•	applicable personas or collectives.

Evaluated for compatibility only.

⸻

Conformance Declaration

Declares:
	•	adherence to required rules, constraints, or standards.

Evaluated against declared requirements; never enforced here.

⸻

Eligibility Declaration

Declares:
	•	which personas, Commons, or contexts are eligible to participate.

Matched across declarations without granting authority.

⸻

Participation Availability Declaration

Declares:
	•	availability of a PeelPack for participation.

This declaration:
	•	makes availability visible,
	•	does not grant permission,
	•	does not route execution,
	•	does not imply willingness or obligation of any participant.

Availability is published; participation is always evaluated and initiated elsewhere.

⸻

Commitment & Provenance Declaration

Declares:
	•	commitments made,
	•	receipts issued,
	•	attestations recorded.

Used for accountability and audit — never control.

⸻

Mediation Semantics

Within this boundary:
	•	Compatibility is evaluated
	•	Eligibility is matched
	•	Permission is scoped
	•	Availability is published

But:
	•	execution is not performed,
	•	discretion is not exercised,
	•	authority is not delegated.

Recipients always evaluate participation options under their own authority.

⸻

Relationship to Execution

Execution may occur only:
	•	downstream of this boundary,
	•	within execution venues already authorized by existing trust and governance boundaries,
	•	and under explicit human or collective control.

IX never executes; it only mediates the conditions under which execution may occur.

⸻

Core Constitutional Invariants
	•	Mediation is singular; implementations may be plural.
	•	Authority remains human and constitutional.
	•	Execution remains downstream and bounded.
	•	Outcomes remain open.
	•	Visibility does not imply permission.
	•	Compatibility does not imply obligation.

⸻

Summary

The IX Mediation Boundary enables plural participation without centralization, without delegation of authority, and without silent control.

It reconciles declarations so that participation can scale —
while preserving human agency, exit, and accountability by design.
