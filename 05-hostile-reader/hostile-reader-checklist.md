HOSTILE READER CHECKLIST
NICE Constitutional System — v0

Purpose
This checklist must be executed before:
• publishing any constitutional artifact
• modifying declarations
• adding or revising standards
• approving platform conformance claims

The checklist is binary.
If any answer is “No” or “Unclear,” the change must not proceed.

⸻

SECTION A — Authority & Origination

□ Does this change clearly preserve the rule that authority originates once — and only once — with the human?

□ Does this change avoid granting any platform, collective, or mediation layer the ability to originate intent, consent, custody, or authorization?

□ Does this change avoid language implying systems “decide,” “determine,” or “act on behalf of” humans?

⸻

SECTION B — Push vs Pull Semantics

□ Does this change avoid any implication that participation is pushed, routed, or delivered as a command?

□ Are all declarations clearly observable or pull-based rather than directive?

□ Is it explicit that recipients evaluate participation under their own authority?

⸻

SECTION C — Assistance vs Delegation

□ Does this change avoid introducing agentic behavior?

□ Does any assistance described refrain from initiating action, narrowing choice, or producing decisions?

□ Is it clear that assistance supports understanding only, not control?

⸻

SECTION D — Sensemaking & Continuity

□ Does this change avoid implying behavioral profiling, prediction, or goal inference?

□ Is continuity with the human described as preservative, not inferential?

□ Is alignment explicitly non-optimizing and non-preemptive?

⸻

SECTION E — Optimization & Nudging

□ Does this change avoid ranking, prioritization, or “best path” defaults?

□ If optimization is mentioned, is it explicitly human-instructed and bounded?

□ Is there no implied preference shaping without explicit human direction?

⸻

SECTION F — Evaluation vs Execution

□ Is evaluation clearly separated from execution?

□ Does this change avoid any implication that mediation triggers execution?

□ Is execution always represented as local, voluntary, and authority-bound?

⸻

SECTION G — Collective Participation

□ Does this change avoid treating collectives as autonomous actors?

□ Are collectives described as governed, not empowered?

□ Is it explicit that collective participation is expressed through humans acting in declared roles?

⸻

SECTION H — Platform Interpretation

□ Does this change avoid framing platforms as owners, gatekeepers, or authorities?

□ Are platforms described as replaceable, conforming implementations?

□ Is custody explicitly human unless otherwise declared?

⸻

SECTION I — Centralization & Power

□ Does this change avoid creating privileged network positions?

□ Is singular enforcement clearly distinguished from centralized control?

□ Are all constraints inspectable and attributable?

⸻

SECTION J — Gatekeeping & Exclusion

□ Does this change avoid silent exclusion or filtering?

□ Are all participation constraints declared and inspectable?

□ Is there no implicit prioritization without declaration?

⸻

SECTION K — Declarative Integrity

□ Are declarations treated as constraints, not instructions?

□ Does this change avoid executable semantics in declaration definitions?

□ Is causality kept outside the constitutional layer?

⸻

SECTION L — Scope Discipline

□ Does this change avoid locking in specific protocols, chains, or execution venues?

□ Are implementation details deferred to standards or lower layers?

□ Is constitutional scope preserved?

⸻

SECTION M — Benevolent Overreach

□ Does this change avoid “for your own good” reasoning?

□ Is human consent always explicit and revocable?

□ Are safety or efficiency arguments constrained by constitutional limits?

⸻

FINAL GATE

□ Would a hostile reader be unable to reasonably claim this change enables:
– inferred intent
– silent execution
– behavioral steering
– hidden authority

□ Would this change survive being implemented by an adversarial platform operator?

□ If misused, would the violation be detectable?

If any box cannot be checked confidently, the change must not be merged.

⸻

Usage Rule
This checklist is mandatory.
Failure to apply it is itself a constitutional violation.
