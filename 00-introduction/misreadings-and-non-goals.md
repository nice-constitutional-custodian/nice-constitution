Common Misreadings and Non-Goals

Purpose

This document clarifies what the NICE Constitution is not.

⸻

Misreading: "This is a product."

The Constitution is not a product.
It defines constraints that products must obey.

⸻

Misreading: "This replaces governance."

The Constitution does not eliminate governance.
It constrains how governance may operate.

⸻

Misreading: "This automates consent."

The Constitution does not automate consent.
It prevents consent from being inferred or substituted.

⸻

Misreading: "This creates collective authority over individuals."

Collectives may coordinate participation.
They may not originate authority.

⸻

Non-Goal: Optimization

The Constitution does not optimize outcomes.
It constrains participation.

⸻

Non-Goal: Ideological Positioning

The Constitution is not ideological.
It is structural.

⸻

Non-Goal: Universal Agreement

The Constitution does not attempt to produce consensus.
It defines conditions under which participation may occur without coercion.
