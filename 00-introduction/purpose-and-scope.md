Purpose and Scope

Constitutional Document — Singular

Purpose

This document defines the purpose and scope of the NICE Constitution.

The NICE Constitution exists to establish durable, structural constraints governing participation in networked systems.

It defines:

• where authority originates  
• what conditions must be satisfied for participation  
• what may never occur at the constitutional layer  
• how collective governance may operate without substituting human standing  

This Constitution does not define products, services, implementations, or technical architectures.
It defines constraints that all such implementations must obey.

⸻

Problem Statement

Modern digital systems optimize for engagement, efficiency, growth, and prediction.

Without structural constraint, optimization mechanisms can:

• substitute interpretation for consent  
• treat default as authorization  
• collapse matching into execution  
• transfer authority to automation  
• convert collective coordination into coercive governance  

The NICE Constitution exists to prevent these failure modes.

⸻

Scope

The Constitution governs participation.

It does not govern:

• specific technologies  
• market outcomes  
• political positions  
• cultural norms  
• application-level design decisions  

It governs only the structural conditions under which participation may occur.

⸻

Non-Delegation Principle

Authority originates with the human.

The Constitution defines the conditions under which authority may be exercised, declared, reconciled, or collectively governed.

It does not originate authority.

⸻

Normative Status

This document is normative, not descriptive.

All platforms, standards, and implementations claiming alignment must conform to its constraints.
