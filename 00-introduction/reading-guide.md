Reading Guide

Purpose

This guide explains how to read the NICE Constitution.

The Constitution is structured by constitutional planes and boundaries, not by features or components.

It should be read in the following order:

1. Purpose and Scope  
2. Participation Map  
3. Plane 1 — Human Authority  
4. Plane 2 — Declared Participation Mediation  
5. Plane 3 — Governed Collective Participation  
6. Constitutional Boundaries  
7. Declarations  
8. Standards  

⸻

How to Interpret "Plane"

A plane is a layer of constitutional constraint.

Planes do not execute actions.
They define conditions under which actions may occur.

⸻

How to Interpret "Boundary"

A boundary prevents one plane from performing the function of another.

Boundaries are anti-capture mechanisms.

If a boundary collapses, power can silently shift.

⸻

How to Interpret "Declaration"

A declaration makes intent, eligibility, availability, or commitment explicit.

Declarations are required so that participation may be evaluated without inference.

⸻

What This Document Does Not Contain

The Constitution does not contain:

• product specifications  
• technical implementation details  
• user experience flows  
• business models  

Those belong to standards and implementations.

⸻

Normative Priority

If a platform or implementation conflicts with the Constitution, the Constitution prevails.
