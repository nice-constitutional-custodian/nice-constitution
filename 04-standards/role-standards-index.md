04-standards/role-standards-index.md

Purpose

This document indexes role standards used to express standing, responsibility, and accountability in NICE.

Role standards define who may declare what, on whose behalf, and under which constraints.

⸻

What qualifies as a Role Standard

A role standard in NICE:
	•	Defines scope of authority, not power
	•	Is explicitly declared and revocable
	•	Is bounded by constitutional constraints
	•	Does not imply hierarchy or privilege

Roles are situational, not identities.

⸻

Role Categories (v0)

The following role categories are reserved:
	1.	Human Roles
	•	Participant
	•	Steward
	•	Delegate (non-authoritative)
	2.	Collective Roles
	•	Commons Steward
	•	Governance Operator
	•	Accountability Reporter
	3.	Platform Roles
	•	Declaration Inspector
	•	Constraint Enforcer
	•	Attestation Provider

⸻

Role Invariants

All roles must be:
	•	Explicitly declared
	•	Scope-bounded
	•	Time-bounded or revocable
	•	Inspectable by affected parties

No role may:
	•	Originate authority
	•	Override human standing
	•	Act without declared scope

⸻

Explicit Non-Coverage

Role standards do not define:
	•	Organizational hierarchy
	•	Employment or legal status
	•	Access control implementation
	•	Credential formats

⸻
RS-HUMAN-PRINCIPAL

Defines:
• baseline human standing
• authority origination rights

Must not:
	•	delegate authority implicitly or by default
	•	be bypassed by platforms, collectives, or automation
	•	have intent inferred or substituted

⸻

RS-COMMONS-STEWARD

Defines:
• authority to declare collective availability
• governance responsibilities

Must not:
• act autonomously
• override participant choice

⸻

RS-PARTICIPANT

Defines:
• eligibility evaluation rights
• voluntary engagement

Must not:
	•	be obligated by visibility, eligibility, or availability
	•	be coerced through defaults, nudges, or implied expectations
	•	lose standing due to non-participation

⸻

RS-OBSERVER

Defines:
• read-only inspection rights
• transparency and audit

Must not:
	•	initiate participation or execution
	•	infer intent, eligibility, or authority
	•	act on observations without explicit declaration

⸻

RS-INFRASTRUCTURE-STEWARD

Defines:
• stewardship without privilege
• maintenance without gatekeeping

Must not:
	•	gain privileged network position or discretionary power
	•	act as a silent gatekeeper
	•	impose policy, interpretation, or participation constraints
